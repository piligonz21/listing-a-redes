Check before you post

  • Photo rights. Make sure the agency or the owner lets you use the photos. If they carry a portal watermark, ask for the originals.
  • Details. Everything in the design comes from the property details. Double-check price, size and rooms with the listing agent.
  • Photos. Check that vertical lines are straight, the light is good and nothing is lying around. Don't retouch anything that changes what the property is like.
  • Contact. The call to action should lead to whoever answers inquiries (your DMs or the agency's WhatsApp).
  • Conditions. If the listing mentions anything special (HOA fees, financing, contingencies), add it to the caption.
